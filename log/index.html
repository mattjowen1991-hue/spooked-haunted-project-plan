Start a new investigation? This clears the marks on this phone. Send your log first if you haven't.

For the editor. Paste in the logs people sent on WhatsApp, all in one go, and they'll be merged into one timeline in time order.

On the night

1. Fill in the location and your name once. They're remembered.

2. At the start of every vigil, do one loud clap in front of both cameras and tap Vigil start / clap. This lets the editor work out exactly where each mark is in the footage.

3. The moment anything happens, tap Mark. The time is saved straight away. Pick what it was and the room, or tap Done and fill it in later by tapping the mark in the list.

4. Use the stars to say how good it was. ★ maybe, ★★ good, ★★★ must use.

5. At the end of the night, tap Send log and send it to the WhatsApp group or straight to Matt.

Good to know

Everything is saved on your phone, so it works with no signal. Closing the page doesn't lose anything.

One person can log for the whole team, or everyone can log on their own phone. The Combine tab merges them all.

Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app: on iPhone, tap Share then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
